Daniel's Story

Remember the Children: Daniel’s Story

2025 / 10 min

I produced and co-directed this animated children’s film with animation director Yaka Hara for the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum. Adapted from the museum’s longstanding exhibition for young people, the film serves as an accessible introduction to the history of the Holocaust. It tells the story of German Jewish boy named Daniel and how he survived the Shoah. We worked closely with historians to ensure historical accuracy and alignment with the original exhibition. The museum released the film as a free educational resource for families and classrooms.
